GUARDIAN SUPPORT — 4 PAGE HTML WEBSITE

Pages:
- index.html: Home
- about.html: About
- services.html: Services
- contact.html: Contact form

To view:
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in Chrome. Keep the assets folder next to the HTML files.

Before publishing:
1. Edit assets/script.js and replace 91XXXXXXXXXX with your WhatsApp number including country code, digits only.
2. Replace support@example.com in the HTML footer and contact page with your real email.
3. Add your actual phone number, business details, team information, service scope and working hours.
4. The form opens WhatsApp with an enquiry message. It does not save data to a database or send email.
5. Add an appropriate privacy notice and verify all claims and support processes before launch.

Emergency disclaimer:
This is a general support website template, not an emergency service, clinical service, legal service, or crisis-response provider.
